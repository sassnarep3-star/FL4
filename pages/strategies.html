2. How an NFL line would be generated (blueprint only)

The following is a proposed calculation design, not a fitted model. No probabilities, fair prices, picks, player estimates, or model correlations have been generated. Official NFL links are discovery/verification routes; they do not establish historical data access or reuse rights.

LG-01

Freeze a point-in-time information set

For each forecast, save the decision cutoff and the source version available by that time. Keep publication time separate from event time; retain revisions instead of overwriting them. Injury reports, roster/participation evidence, player statistics, and schedule/results are discovery sources only until rights and historical coverage are established.

X(t0) = {records with verified source-time <= forecast cutoff t0}

LG-02

Fit a joint game-score distribution, not just a point estimate

Define targets and features before fitting. A candidate model would estimate a distribution over home/away scores conditional on point-in-time team, opponent, role, and context features. Derive win/cover/total probabilities by summing the distribution under each market's exact settlement rules; do not substitute a mean margin for a cover probability. No model or data has been fitted here.

D = home points - away points;  T = home points + away points
P(D=d, T=u | X(t0))

LG-03

Convert probabilities to fair single-market prices

For each specific contract (moneyline, spread, or total), calculate win, push/void, and loss probabilities using the applicable market line and settlement rule. A fair price is a break-even transformation, not a prediction of a posted book price. At an offered price, compare expected value only when the price timestamp, settlement, and costs are known. The three-state formula below applies only to full win / refund / full loss settlement; partial-payout contracts require expected payout over every settlement state. Exact point-spread/total break-even thresholds must handle discrete ties and pushes.

pW + pP + pL = 1
Fair decimal odds O* = 1 + pL/pW = (1 - pP)/pW
EV per unit at offered decimal O = pW(O - 1) - pL

LG-04

Model player opportunities and replacement roles separately

For props, separate opportunity (routes, snaps, carries, targets, or defensive participation where verified) from conditional production. Track the injured/absent starter and possible second-, third-, and fourth-string replacements on both offense and defense. Assign a tier only when an official source explicitly establishes it; an NFL roster page is not automatically a depth chart. Keep injury status, role, participation, forecast cutoff, observed snaps, and replacement-effect estimate blank until each is sourced and tested. Do not infer deficiency from an absence count.

P(player result >= k | verified role, opportunities, availability at t0, opponent/context)
Replacement effect = measured out-of-sample change vs. a preregistered baseline

LG-05

Price parlays and prop combos from joint outcomes

Estimate the joint event probability under shared game and usage context. A marginal correlation coefficient describes association but is not the probability of a multi-leg settlement. Multiply marginal probabilities only if conditional independence is justified and validated for the relevant joint events. Apply each leg's push, void, and settlement rules to the joint outcomes; report uncertainty.

P(A1 and ... and An | X(t0))
Do not replace this with product_i P(Ai | X(t0)) unless independence is supported

LG-06

Separate predictive skill from luck before a leaderboard

Pre-register the forecast target, baseline, feature set, metric, model version, evaluation period, exclusions, and decision rule. Use chronological walk-forward or held-out seasons; score probabilities with proper scoring/calibration measures, and report return only as a separate, net-of-price measure. Quantify uncertainty at the game/week/season cluster level where appropriate, address repeated strategy searches, and preserve a final untouched holdout. No threshold is selected and no test is run.

Freeze protocol -> chronological evaluation -> calibration / proper score -> uncertainty & multiplicity review -> independent replication
