Correlation

How dependence between legs will be measured, and the refusal that stands in place of a number until it is.

Every value in this table is null, and that is the finding
The brief asks for correlation values to be determined. Nothing here has been measured, because a correlation between two settled legs requires a settlement ledger and this project has never settled a bet. The alternative - importing textbook numbers - would produce exactly the confident, uncorroborated figures this project exists to avoid. So the register ships with the protocol, the sample minimum, and blanks. Until a pair is measured, parlays containing it are refused, not priced with assumed independence.

Measurement protocol

  • Unit: paired legs settled on the same game.
  • Primary statistic: phi coefficient on the 2x2 joint settlement table, plus the excess over independence: p(A and B) − p(A)p(B), which is the quantity that actually enters parlay pricing.
  • Secondary: Spearman rank correlation for continuous outcomes.
  • Interval: nonparametric bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, 95% interval; if the interval covers the independence value the pair is labelled 'no detectable dependence' and earns no correlation credit.
  • Stratification: by leg family and by pre-game favourite-margin bucket, because a blowout induces dependence a close game does not.
  • Pitfall recorded so it is not rediscovered: computing correlation on wins and losses of our bets instead of on event outcomes folds our own pricing errors into the dependence estimate. Measure events, apply to prices.

Portfolio consequences

Finding
A parlay is only worth its correlation credit if the credit exceeds the extra vig. Compute both, publish both, and refuse the bet when the credit is unmeasured - which today is every bet in this catalogue.
Same-game legs share the referee, the weather, the game script and the availability report. The portfolio view must therefore cap exposure per driver, not per bet; S-01, S-02, S-03, S-07 and S-08 all read from the same injury file, so five strategies agreeing is one bet, not five.
Correlation between our strategies is more decision-relevant than correlation between two legs, because capital allocation follows strategy diversification.
