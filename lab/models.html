Origination order

Originate the side first, then the total, then the moneyline. Why: The side and the total are the primitives; a moneyline is a function of the same distribution. Deriving the moneyline last keeps one distribution behind all three markets so they cannot disagree with each other. Consequence if violated: Three independently fitted markets can imply three different games, and the disagreement is invisible until a parlay prices it. Frozen: True

Output contract

Every model output is stored as a probability plus its inputs, never as a pick alone. A pick without its inputs cannot be audited, cannot be re-run after a data correction, and cannot be asked what it would have said under a different availability state.

Two-sided. The brief asks for a two-sided 'what would change our mind' entry. Both directions are required: evidence that would strengthen the strategy and evidence that would kill it. A one-sided entry is a sales pitch, and the builder rejects it.

Ablations, not fixed penalties

Injury and personnel effects are measured by ablation, never by subtracting a fixed number of points for a missing starter. A fixed penalty is a guess wearing a number. It cannot be falsified, it does not vary by position or by the quality of the replacement, and it silently becomes a permanent prior once it is in the code. Status: Protocol published. 0 ablations run, because verified availability features do not exist yet (Q-DEPTH-01).

Step
1. Fit the identical model twice on the identical training window: once with the availability/personnel feature set, once without it.
2. Evaluate both on chronological holdouts, never on a random split, so no fold trains on the future.
3. Report the difference in the proper score, with an interval from a game-clustered bootstrap.
4. If the interval contains zero, the correct published statement is 'no measured effect at this sample size', not a point estimate.

Injury signal form

The injury signal is a surprise, never a level.

surprise = designation observed at time t minus the expected designation for that player in that game week, where the expectation is built only from observations made strictly before t.

Why a level fails: A level says 'this player is Questionable'. A surprise says 'this player is Questionable when we expected him to be a full participant'. Only the second is new information, and only the second is not already priced by anyone reading the same report.

Status: Definition published. 0 surprise values are computable from a single capture (Q-SNAPSHOT-01).

2026 regime priors

2026 kickoff and declared onside-kick regime priors must be estimated from 2026 results only. The regime change is confirmed from the official rulebook; the rates under it are not measured, so every prior is null rather than carried over from a season played under different rules.
