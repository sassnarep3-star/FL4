Correlations

Eleven pairs registered, zero measured. The combo divisions stay closed until the joint evidence exists.

Protocol published, zero values measured. Every measured_value below is null because there is no set of settled bets to measure on (Q-PRICE-01, Q-PRICE-03).

Policy

Correlations are computed only from officially sourced numbers, and n is published with every value.

  • What counts as an official input — Settlement outcomes and market prices taken from a licensed or permitted source. Model probabilities are not an official number and are never used to estimate a correlation.
  • Why n is published — A correlation without n is unfalsifiable. At n=30 nearly any value is consistent with zero, and publishing it invites exactly the parlay pricing it should forbid.
  • Minimum n — 300 (Proposed default. The owner may raise it; the builder refuses to render a value below whatever floor is set (Q-PRICE-03).)
  • Clustering — Bets on the same game are not independent. Intervals come from a bootstrap clustered by game, not from an i.i.d. formula.
  • What a correlation is not — A correlation coefficient is not a joint probability, not a conditional probability and not a causal effect. It does not license multiplying marginals.

Measurement plan

Step
1. Answer Q-PRICE-01 and Q-PRICE-03 so there is a price series and an agreed n floor.
2. Build the settlement ledger: every bet, its market, its price at forecast time, its closing price, and its outcome, keyed by game_id.
3. Estimate phi and the excess joint probability for each pair, with a game-clustered bootstrap interval.
4. Publish n alongside every value. A pair below the floor is published as 'not measured at this n', never as a number.
5. Only then may the joint-evidence gate open for that specific pair, and only for that pair.

Informational research only. Nothing here is betting advice. No market price exists in this environment, so this section publishes no odds, no edge, no closing-line value and no return number. Every row links to a source; values that could not be confirmed are left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
