Price policy

The legal basis, the policy options and the store specification — published before any price exists.

This page exists because most of the brief is blocked behind one question. It states the legal basis verbatim, the policy options, the store specification and the publication schedule — all of which can be published before a single price exists, and none of which are useful if they are written after one arrives.

What this site does because of it

  • No automated league capture is configured or scheduled anywhere in this repository.
  • The captures on this site were read page by page during a review session and are published as a dated snapshot with links, not as a maintained feed.
  • No market price, odds, line or closing number is stored or published, because none was obtained from a source with a licence to redistribute it.
  • Raw row-level capture data remains in the published repository tree, which is a violation risk under the public/private split requirement. It is flagged (L-F-04) rather than presented as compliant.

Vig-free decomposition

Separate genuine pricing divergence from operator margin before any bet structure is considered.

Step
1. Convert each book's prices to implied probabilities using that book's own price format; do not average prices, average probabilities.
2. Normalise the implied probabilities within a market so they sum to 1. The excess is the book's overround, i.e. the vig.
3. The vig-free consensus is the mean of the normalised probabilities across books, weighted by a rule set before the first capture (equal weighting is the default and the safest).
4. The originated fair line is produced by our own model, independently of the books.
5. Residual = originated fair probability minus vig-free consensus probability, per market.
6. Publish the residual, the vig removed, and the number of books behind the consensus, as three separate columns.

What is not done: No book count, no vig estimate and no consensus value is published, because no book price exists in this environment.

Failure mode guarded: Comparing an originated price to a single book's raw price measures the book's margin, not our skill. The decomposition exists so that mistake cannot be made silently.

Residual publication schedule

Price a line ourselves and publish the residual against the market, on a schedule, from day one.

One residual row per originated market per fixture, published before kickoff, and stored immutably. Two rows per market minimum: the forecast-time price and the closing price.

Status: Schedule and schema published. 0 rows, blocked on Q-PRICE-01.

Day-one rule: The residual must be published for the first originated week, including the weeks where the residual is unfavourable. A residual series that starts after a good week is a selection artefact.
