Decision readiness

What is verified, what is blocked, and the gate that blocks it. Nothing on this page is inferred.

This section is one answer to a question the brief keeps asking: what can we actually decide today, and what are we pretending to decide? Everything below is published whether or not it flatters the project. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source it is blank, and the blank is listed on the Flagged for Review page with the reason and a link.

Executive summary — the important details

  1. The verified layer is complete for 2026 weeks 1–10. 148 FINAL games, each linked to its official game centre, plus 347 rows of the official Week 10 injury report across 28 clubs and venue/stadium/Game Book links for all 14 Week 10 fixtures. Every one of those rows carries a source link to nfl.com or operations.nfl.com.
  2. The performance layer is empty, and that is the finding rather than the gap. No odds, line, closing number or book exists anywhere in this environment. Closing-line value, backtests, ROI, correlations of settled legs and originated residuals are all specified and all unmeasurable. This site publishes no edge number, and the builder fails if one ever reaches a page.
  3. 12 blocking gates are open, and most of them are one question. 3 of the 12 are the same issue from different angles — where a price comes from, what the terms permit, and what may be kept. They are decisions, not tasks, and each one has a named default if it is never answered.
  4. Depth is the largest unmet requirement. 0 of 32 clubs have a verified depth source, so 0 replacements are named anywhere on this site and the Offense vs Defense Matchup Deficiency Matrix ships with every cell blank. It is published empty on purpose: filling it from jersey number, experience or salary is explicitly rejected, and an empty grid makes the cost visible.
  5. Two rejections are recorded as standing rules, not preferences. Depth order is never derived from jersey number, experience or salary, and strategies are never ranked by season-long ROI. Both are the fastest ways to fill a column and the most reliable ways to be confidently wrong.
  6. The governance layer is published before there is anything to govern. The pre-registration schema, the two-stage qualification, the control baselines, the demotion triggers and the anti-gaming rules all exist with thresholds deliberately unset, so every gate fails closed. A threshold chosen after the first result is not a gate.
  7. Three things are known to be wrong and are published rather than fixed silently. Three captures of the same official injury report disagree on row counts; the injury capture has one truncated row; and raw capture data currently sits in the tree GitHub Pages publishes. All three are on the Flagged for Review page with links.
  8. Nothing here is betting advice and no money is involved. Rewards are non-cash, no capital is exposed at any stage, and the leaderboard can only score bets registered before the settlement they are scored against.
