Signal strategies

Sixteen ways to generate signals for originating lines, each with its inputs, its vetoes, and how it loses.

What this catalogue is, and what it is not
Sixteen candidate signal generators for originating prices. Each one is written as a testable claim with the falsifying evidence named, so a strategy can be killed by data rather than defended by narrative. No edge number appears anywhere, and edge_claim is null on every row - not because the ideas are weak but because there is no price data in this repo with which to compute one. Publishing a plausible percentage here would be the fastest way to destroy the value of the entire exercise.

Families, and why they are separated

The families are not cosmetic. A news-latency strategy is a race against a market's repricing speed and is measured in minutes; a unit-quality strategy is a modelling claim and is measured in weeks of residuals; a portfolio strategy is a pricing-arithmetic claim and needs correlation, not forecasts; and execution (S-12, the shopping baseline) is the number all the others have to beat before anyone spends money on them. Mixing them on one scoreboard is how a system ends up rewarding whoever posted most often.

The most important entry in the table is S-12, the pure line-shopping baseline: it has no opinion about football and exists so that every modelled edge can be compared with the cheapest available edge. If a strategy cannot beat S-12's CLV at the same stake, it is not a signal, it is a story.

Design decisions embedded in this catalogue

DecisionReason
Every strategy names its own no-bet conditionsA signal without an abstention rule is a betting compulsion with a model attached. The vetoes are the part that survives contact with reality.
How it loses is mandatory, next to why it might be realSG-18. Optimism should not be able to travel alone on this site.
Prop strategies carry the missing input in the first column, not a footnoteSo nobody inherits them as 'ready'.
Strategies are not allowed to share an input without sharing an exposure capS-01/S-02/S-03/S-07/S-08 all read the same injury file; five signals agreeing is one bet, which is what G-8 enforces.
The shopping baseline is on the board as a competitorThe leaderboard should be able to embarrass the models, that is the point of a competition.
